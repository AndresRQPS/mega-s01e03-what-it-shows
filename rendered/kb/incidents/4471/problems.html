#4471 · what the merged reports really hold

owner support desk open reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

#4471 was marked known: one issue, one fix queued. It was not one issue. The desk merged on subject line (decision 0001), and every report about a wrong date looks the same from its subject. Read by tenant, channel and body, the reports split into separate problems. Each needs its own fix, so each has its own ticket.

#4471 is closed, but not every problem in it is. The one-day move has no fix and stays open.

Where each problem stands

Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October 2026.

  • parser: fixed. fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Issue 1 is closed.
  • hint: won’t fix. The booking-v3 form is retired, so its hint goes with it. Issue 2 is closed as not planned.
  • one-day: open. The engineering update does not mention it, and neither change touches time zones. Issue 3 stays open. Send new one-day reports there, not to #4471.

How to tell them apart

parser: the shared parser reads month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first. The parser reads 07/04 as 4 July, not 7 April, so day and month swap. It comes through every channel: app, email, and the booking-v2 form, whose hint already says DD/MM/YYYY. Fix: parse by the tenant’s locale. Fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.

hint: the booking-v3 form asks for the wrong order. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. The tenant’s people type month first, as told, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. Day and month swap again, but only through the form, and only for tenants on booking-v3. The parser fix does not touch it. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired. If a form like it comes back, its hint must follow the tenant’s locale.

one-day: dates move by one day, not by a swap. A Tuesday shows as the Monday before. One client in Lisbon and one in Toronto got different days for the same meeting; an invite arrived before its booking was made; one tenant sees it only in June. That points at time zones, not at day and month order. Neither change above touches it, so it is still open. Fix: find where the booking’s time zone is lost between booking and invite.

What intake tagged

Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser, the moment it arrives. The tag is a guess: it does not know the tenant or the form. The intake tagged as column above shows how often the guess is wrong. Do not split or count by intake tag.

Lessons

  • A matching subject line is not the same cause. Before you mark a big ticket known, place a sample of its reports by tenant, channel and body.
  • A report that does not fit the pattern is a problem of its own until shown otherwise. Count it, ticket it, do not footnote it.
